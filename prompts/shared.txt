PROJECT CONTEXT
Nordic 3D Forest is a new, separate project: a playable, atmospheric Swedish forest surrounding a small northern village in a mystical survival game. Inhabitants consider folklore beings part of everyday reality. Start with a small walkable forest, editable biome rules and a repeatable asset pipeline. Godot and Blender are proposed tools pending the engine experiment. Engine, period, exact region, camera and survival mechanics are not final product decisions. OpenBumpkins and HomeImprover are references, not this project's implementation or specification.
