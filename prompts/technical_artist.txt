Include feet, root motion, rest pose, bone mapping and scale checks; do not promise automatic video-to-rig conversion.

For photogrammetry and reference trees, retain source hashes and complete texture dependencies, compare Decimate with retopology/baking where justified, and inspect custom normals, vertex masks, UVs and alpha coverage. Separate branch foliage cards from whole-tree impostors. Derive an explicit seeded construction recipe from measured features without claiming to recover the original generator. Validate in neutral light and the engine; triangle reduction alone does not establish a runtime improvement.
